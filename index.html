About

I'm a third-year Computer Science student driven by curiosity about how software and physical systems interact. My focus sits at the intersection of code and the real world — automation, electronics, and the systems that connect the factory floor to the enterprise.

I'm drawn to problems where software has a direct, tangible impact: machines that move, systems that respond, and processes that run reliably at scale.

Currently building my skills and project portfolio as I work toward a career in industrial software or controls engineering.
